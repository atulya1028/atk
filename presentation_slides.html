PATIENT PROTOCOL 3-STAGE ASSESSMENT FUNNEL

Adaptive Assessment Flow

Optimizes respondent engagement while capturing deep clinical data.

Stage 1

Demographics

Age, Gender, Postcode, Occupation, and Contact details to establish baseline records and territory routing.

Stage 2

Initial Screening

High-level preliminary questionnaire. If cumulative score meets qualification threshold (e.g. ≥ 30), Stage 3 unlocks.

Stage 3

Detailed Evaluation

In-depth clinical inquiry with conditional logic branching. Automatically triggers critical risk alerts if score ≥ 50.
